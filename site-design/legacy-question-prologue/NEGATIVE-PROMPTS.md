# Negative Prompts

Use these negative prompts across the full Legacy Question cinematic prologue.

## Global Negative Prompt

No text, no logos, no watermarks, no QR codes, no barcodes, no fake UI, no app interface, no holograms, no neon, no fantasy portals, no magic, no haunted styling, no horror lighting, no clear grieving faces, no theatrical crying, no deformed hands, no duplicate fingers, no extra fingers, no missing fingers, no malformed anatomy, no angels, no ghosts, no skeletons, no demons, no religious iconography, no heroic poses, no superhero trailer style, no orange-and-teal blockbuster grading, no oversaturated gold, no generic SaaS design, no stock-photo grief, no smiling at camera, no fantasy castle, no spaceships, no astronauts, no sci-fi fonts, no generated typography, no readable signage, no corporate inspiration mood, no sentimental commercial piano mood.

## Funeral Scenes

Avoid horror cemetery imagery, haunted graveyard, lightning, dramatic crying faces, visible corpse, ornate fantasy mausoleum, angel statues as focal point, skeletons, excessive fog, theatrical grief, stock funeral photography.

## Photograph Scenes

Avoid readable handwriting, QR codes, barcodes, identifiable faces, modern glossy photo booth prints, phone screens, fake UI overlays, perfect clean paper, exaggerated damage, horror blood stains, deformed hands, duplicate fingers.

## Absence Scenes

Avoid haunted-house interiors, horror shadows, jump-scare composition, floating objects, glowing spirits, cluttered melodrama, visible brand logos, readable book titles, generated text, overly warm cozy lifestyle styling.

## Mansion Scenes

Avoid fantasy castles, haunted mansions, Disney-like estates, magical portals, glowing runes, lightning storms, bats, overgrown ruin, horror silhouettes, sci-fi structures, modern glass hotel, bright gold floodlighting.

## QR Reveal Scenes

Avoid real QR codes, readable codes, fake UI, glowing hologram panels, neon scan effects, phone screen overlays, cyberpunk grids, laser beams, magic symbols, excessive particle effects.

## Memory Montage

Avoid collage layouts, floating objects, stock-photo smiles, clear faces looking at camera, readable letters, fake app screens, corporate productivity desk, modern SaaS dashboard, generic scrapbook design.

## Road and Question Scenes

Avoid fantasy roads, portal roads, glowing runes, video game quest markers, heroic figure poses, horror forest, monster silhouettes, bright moon fantasy, neon path, overly symmetrical poster art.

## Claim and Confirmation

Avoid confetti, celebration graphics, cheerful app-success visuals, bright green checkmarks, corporate onboarding style, fake inbox UI, readable email text, exaggerated gold explosion, superhero reveal.
