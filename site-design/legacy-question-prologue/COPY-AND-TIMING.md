## Interaction Rules

- Continue during prologue completes the current line or beat, then advances to the next scene boundary.
- Continue must not skip a half-rendered phrase abruptly.
- Skip Prologue jumps directly to the coded question, pauses all prologue media, and does not mark the answer as preserved.
- Typing, focusing the answer field, starting recording, or submitting pauses cinematic progression immediately.
- Hidden browser tabs pause video effects, timers, and audio stems.
- The real answer-preserved copy appears only after confirmed server success.
- The claim confirmation copy appears only after the claim email flow succeeds.
- Audio fades under assistive technology announcements and form errors.

## Copy Placement Rules

- Desktop maximum line width: 34 characters for title lines, 54 characters for supporting lines.
- Mobile maximum line width: 22-28 characters depending on viewport.
- Keep copy off faces, hands, photograph details, QR plate area, and mansion windows.
- Use code-rendered text only, with accessible HTML.
- Preserve line breaks from `FINAL-COPY.md` unless the mobile layout requires a shorter line break.
