## Continuity Problems

- Mourner appearance: `reference-mourner-neutral-sheet.png` is the best anchor. `scene-03-mourner-alone-with-photograph-alt-02.png` shows too much face and should not be used. Some scene mourners have different hair volume and coat silhouettes; keep the rear/side anonymous shots only.
- Photograph design: `reference-photograph-angled-view.png`, `reference-photograph-wet-version.png`, and the Scene 2 master photo images align best. `reference-photograph-front-view.png` and `scene-02-hands-holding-photograph-alt-01.png` show the pictured person too clearly. `scene-05-light-touching-photograph-master.png` drifts toward a stacked album/object rather than the single canonical photograph.
- Mansion architecture: Candidate sheet 02 and the Scene 4 reveal risk a more castle/haunted read. `scene-09-additional-mansion-window-illuminated-master.png` is the strongest approved mansion anchor. Lock future mansion images to that architecture and reduce fog-horror in Scene 4 if revised.
- Road geography: The road evolves from cemetery valley to dark forest road to estate driveway. This can work narratively, but `scene-07-road-emerging-through-darkness-alt-01.png` is the least consistent with the forest/estate road geography.
- Lighting progression: The sequence mostly works: cold funeral/interior, then amber light, then road illumination. Watch `scene-08-empty-question-environment-master.png` and final Scene 9 images for amber becoming too bright too early.
- Haunted/magical/fantasy risks: Highest risk backup/reference images are `scene-04-first-mansion-reveal-master.png`, `reference-mansion-candidate-sheet-02.png`, and the darker mansion fog candidates. The active corrected first reveal is approved. QR plate images are clean and not magical.
