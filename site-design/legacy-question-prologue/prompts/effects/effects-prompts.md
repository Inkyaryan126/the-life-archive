## Reduced-Motion Rules

- Disable foreground video effects or freeze them at low opacity.
- Replace road, QR, and window reveals with short opacity fades.
- Keep all copy readable without motion.
- Never let effect playback block typing, recording, or submission.
- Pause all visual effects when the browser tab is hidden.
