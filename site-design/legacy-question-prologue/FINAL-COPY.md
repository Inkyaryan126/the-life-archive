# Final Copy

## Preferred Copy

```text
Somewhere in the future…
someone may need your words.

A photograph can preserve a face.
It can’t answer what was left unsaid.

What would you give
to hear their voice again?

To hear what they never got to say?

Death doesn’t have to take every word.
Some voices can still find their way home.

When they come searching…
let them find more than a photograph.

A voice. A story.
Something only you could have said.

The doors are still ahead.
The first key is one honest answer.

WHAT WOULD YOU WANT THEM TO KNOW?

If the people you love could hear one message from you after you’re gone, what would you say?
```

## After Confirmed Submission

```text
Your first answer has been preserved.
Now give it a permanent place.

CLAIM YOUR ANSWER

Enter your email and we’ll send you a secure path back to the words you just left behind.

CLAIM THE BEGINNING

Your answer stays private. We won’t publish it or sell your information.
```

## After Claim Email Confirmation

```text
THE PATH IS OPEN.

Your secure way back is waiting in your email.

The archive is waiting.

CONTINUE TO THE DOORS
```

## Capitalization Rules

- Use sentence case for cinematic copy.
- Use all caps only for the main question, claim heading, final state heading, and final button.
- Do not title-case emotional sentences.
- Button labels may use all caps only when visually restrained with accessible text.

## Punctuation Rules

- Use the single ellipsis character shown in the preferred copy.
- Keep contractions with curly apostrophes: `can’t`, `doesn’t`, `we’ll`, `won’t`, `you’re`.
- Do not add exclamation points.
- Do not add decorative punctuation or quotation marks.

## Line-Break Rules

- Preserve the line breaks shown above for desktop unless composition requires one additional break.
- Mobile may break long supporting lines earlier, but must not change wording.
- Keep the main question on one or two lines depending on viewport.
- Do not orphan small words like `a`, `to`, or `the` on their own line unless matching the intended poetic break.

## Maximum Line Width

- Desktop title/cinematic line: 34 characters.
- Desktop supporting copy: 54 characters.
- Mobile title/cinematic line: 22-28 characters.
- Mobile supporting copy: 30-36 characters.

## Sizing Guidance

- Desktop cinematic copy: 40-64px depending on scene density, line-height 1.05-1.18.
- Desktop supporting copy: 18-24px, line-height 1.45-1.65.
- Mobile cinematic copy: 28-38px, line-height 1.08-1.2.
- Mobile supporting copy: 16-18px, line-height 1.45-1.6.
- Buttons: large enough for 44px minimum touch target.

## Accessibility Wording

- Screen readers should receive the same copy in logical order.
- Do not announce decorative scene changes unless they affect user understanding.
- The answer field label should be explicit: `Your message`.
- The submit button should describe action: `Preserve my answer`.
- The claim email field label should be explicit: `Email address`.

## Screen-Reader Text

- Prologue region label: `Legacy Question introduction`.
- Skip control: `Skip introduction and go to the question`.
- Continue control: `Continue introduction`.
- Question region label: `Legacy Question`.
- Confirmation region label: `Answer preserved`.
- Final confirmation label: `Path is open`.

## Reduced-Motion Copy Behavior

- Copy fades in without blur, parallax, or long animated delays.
- Each thought still appears in sequence unless the user skips.
- The question appears immediately after the final prologue thought.
- Confirmed submission and claim states remain gated by real server success.
